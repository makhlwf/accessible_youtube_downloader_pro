# Privacy Policy for HexPlayer

**Last Updated: May 24, 2024**

HexPlayer is an open-source, accessible YouTube downloader and player. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to your privacy and the handling of any data while using the application.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
HexPlayer is designed as a local-first application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information to our servers or any third parties, except as required for the core functionality of the application (interacting with YouTube).

## 2. YouTube Cookies and Authentication
If you choose to use the "Cookies File Path" feature in the settings:
- The provided cookies file is used exclusively by the local instance of HexPlayer to authenticate your requests with YouTube.
- This allows you to access features like your watch history, recommendations, and age-restricted content.
- Your cookies are **never** uploaded to our servers, shared with the developer, or transmitted to any third party other than Google/YouTube.
- The path to your cookies file is stored in your local configuration file (`settings.ini`).

## 3. Local Data Storage
HexPlayer stores the following information locally on your device:
- **Settings:** Your preferences (language, download path, etc.) are stored in `settings.ini` within your local AppData folder.
- **Favorites:** If you save videos to your favorites, this list is stored in a local SQLite database on your device.
- **Watch History:** If enabled via cookies, your YouTube watch history is managed by YouTube's servers. HexPlayer itself may cache search results locally for performance.

## 4. Third-Party Services
HexPlayer interacts with the following third-party services:
- **YouTube/Google:** When you search for or play videos, requests are sent to YouTube. Your use of these features is subject to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **GitHub:** When you check for updates, the app connects to GitHub to compare versions.
- **Deno & yt-dlp:** The app may download these open-source tools from their respective GitHub repositories to enable core features.

## 5. No Analytics or Tracking
HexPlayer does **not** contain any analytics, telemetry, or tracking scripts. We do not monitor how you use the app or what content you choose to watch or download.

## 6. Transparency and Open Source
As an open-source project, our source code is publicly available for audit. You can verify how we handle your data by reviewing our code on [GitHub](https://github.com/makhlwf/accessible_youtube_downloader_pro).
